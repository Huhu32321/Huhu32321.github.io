About

I grew up in Beijing near the ruins of Yuanmingyuan — a place where you can only imagine what once stood there. That made me curious about how technology can let people step back into spaces they cannot otherwise visit, and ask questions about them.

My MSc dissertation at Trinity College Dublin explored exactly that: reconstructing heritage sites on campus with 3D Gaussian Splatting and letting visitors explore them through natural conversation with an LLM guide. Before that, I worked on real-time object detection in industry and published on path planning for an underwater robot fish.

I'm now looking for a PhD where I can keep working at the intersection of neural 3D reconstruction, human–AI interaction and extended reality.
