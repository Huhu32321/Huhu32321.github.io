Hi, I'm Yuhui.

MSc Computer Science (AR/VR), Trinity College Dublin — Distinction.

I grew up in Beijing near the ruins of Yuanmingyuan — a place you can only imagine as it once stood. That made me curious about how technology can let people step back into spaces they cannot otherwise visit, and ask questions about them.

My MSc dissertation did exactly that: reconstructing Trinity College heritage sites with 3D Gaussian Splatting and letting visitors explore them by talking to an LLM guide. Before that I built real-time detection systems in industry and published on path planning for a robot fish.

I'm looking for a PhD starting in 2027 at the intersection of neural 3D reconstruction, human–AI interaction and extended reality.
