Research

I'm interested in making reconstructed 3D environments explorable through natural language: how photoreal neural representations such as 3D Gaussian Splatting can be grounded with LLMs, rendered in real time, and evaluated with people.

Conversational Immersive Scene Exploration: Integrating 3D Gaussian Splatting Reconstruction and LLM-Driven Natural Language Interaction for Cultural Heritage Visualization
Yuhui Cao, supervised by Michael Manzke
MSc Dissertation, Trinity College Dublin, 2026 · Distinction
pdf / video

Photoreal 3DGS reconstructions of Trinity College sites (Old Library Long Room, Front Square and more) paired with a per-scene LLM guide. Evaluated on reconstruction fidelity (PSNR / SSIM / LPIPS), rendering performance and a 40-question conversational grounding benchmark.
